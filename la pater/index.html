Our Story

A Passion Born in
the Heart of Madras

Nestled in the vibrant streets of Chennai, La Patisserie Madras is where the art of French baking meets the warmth of Tamil Nadu. Every creation is a love letter — from buttery croissants kissed by morning sun to towering celebration cakes that mark life's sweetest moments.

Our master pâtissiers blend time-honored French techniques with locally sourced ingredients, crafting confections that are as breathtaking to behold as they are to taste.
